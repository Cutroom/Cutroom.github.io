Privacy at Cutroom

Last updated July 23, 2026

Sandbox status

Cutroom is currently a private development sandbox available only to approved test users.

Account authorization

Cutroom uses platform-provided OAuth authorization. It does not request or store a user’s social-media password. Authorization tokens are stored in the local macOS Keychain on the app owner’s device.

Media and publishing

Source videos and generated clips are processed and stored locally. A clip is not queued for upload unless a reviewer approves it and automatic upload has been explicitly enabled for the destination account.

Access and deletion

A connected account can be disconnected from Cutroom at any time, which removes its saved authorization from the local Keychain and stops future uploads.
